How to Interpret Technical Stock Charts

By TradeMind AI Team · Published 2026-10-11 · Updated 2026-10-11

Start with the trend

Zoom out first. An uptrend is a sequence of higher highs and higher lows; a downtrend is lower highs and lower lows; anything else is a range. Decide which one you are looking at before adding indicators.

Candlesticks

Each candle shows open, high, low and close for one period. A long body means a decisive move. Long wicks mean price went somewhere and was rejected.

Support and resistance

Support is a price area where buyers have previously stepped in; resistance is where sellers have. They are zones, not exact lines, and they weaken each time they are tested.

Volume

A breakout on rising volume is generally more convincing than one on thin volume. Volume does not make a breakout safe; it only adds context.

Timeframe matters

A pattern on a 5-minute chart and one on a weekly chart are not comparable in importance. Check at least two timeframes before drawing conclusions.
