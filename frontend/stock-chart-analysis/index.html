From chart to report

Reading a chart by eye is subjective. After studying the picture, run the computed report to check what RSI, MACD, moving averages and volatility say numerically.

Alerts and backtests

Turn an idea into an alert rule, or test a simple strategy on historical candles in the backtesting engine. Backtests show how a rule behaved in the past and do not predict the future.
