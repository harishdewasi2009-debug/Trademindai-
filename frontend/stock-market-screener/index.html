Stock Market Screener for India

The screener lets you browse the full NSE and BSE equity universe and MCX commodities, search any instrument instantly, and open a computed report for the one you want to study.

Browse or search

  • Paginated browser across NSE, BSE and MCX.
  • Instant search by name or symbol with live prices in the dropdown.
  • Choose the time interval used for the computed signals.

Breakout scanner

A separate scanner looks for breakout and pattern setups on real candles. Pick how much of the universe to scan, pick a scan type, or search one stock to see its breakdown across every scan type.

Access

Paid plans include the screener. Free accounts get a 7-day trial from signup. Signing in is required to use it.

Stock market screening tools with breakout analysis

Use the stock market screening tools to narrow a large universe, then open the computed report to verify a candidate on the chart. The breakout scanner covers breakout analysis and pattern setups on real candles. A screen is a starting list for research, not a list of stocks to buy.
