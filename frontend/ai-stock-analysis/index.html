AI Stock Analysis for Indian Markets

TradeMind AI combines computed technical indicators with large-language-model commentary so you can read a stock's chart structure in plain language. Pick an NSE or BSE stock, run the analysis, and get a structured report in seconds.

How the analysis works

Every report starts with real candle data from the Upstox market feed. RSI, MACD, EMA, VWAP, Supertrend, ATR and Bollinger Bands are calculated by code, not guessed by a model.

The AI layer then reads those computed numbers and explains them: trend, momentum, support and resistance zones, volatility, and the main risks to the reading. The numbers come first; the language model only describes them.

Analysis modes

  • Quick analysis for a single NSE or BSE stock or MCX commodity.
  • Compare mode to put two instruments side by side on the same indicators.
  • Deep Research that combines the daily and weekly timeframe in one read.
  • Multi-model analysis on higher plans, so you can see where different models agree or differ.

What it is not

AI-generated analysis can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It does not predict prices and it is not a buy, sell or hold recommendation. TradeMind AI is not registered with SEBI as a Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. Use the output as one input to your own research.
